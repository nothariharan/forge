On your side, under your control

FORGE is built on Omnigent. Omnigent spawns and governs the agents; FORGE adds the scientific workflow and the record.

Omnigent orchestrates

Spawns the director and seven specialists, holds their sessions, and enforces policy in the engine itself: a dispatch and experiment budget (P2) and a tool allowlist that applies to every sub-agent (P5).

FORGE validates

Every handoff is checked against the event schema before it is recorded. A malformed one is rejected and logged with its raw input, and the engine blocks the next dispatch until it is fixed.

Humans decide

The lab and the command line only read the ledger and send decisions back. Experiments wait on a human approval (P6), and agents can never write one themselves.

Meet the agents
